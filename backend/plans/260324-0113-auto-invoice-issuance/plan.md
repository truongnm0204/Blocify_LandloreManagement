# Plan: Tu dong phat hanh hoa don + UI nang cap

## Tong quan
Tu dong tao (generate) va phat hanh (issue) hoa don cho cac hop dong ACTIVE theo chu ky thanh toan (MONTHLY/QUARTERLY/YEARLY). Dien nuoc va phi dich vu van tinh hang thang.
Nang cap UI: manual generate invoice, invoice list group by motel voi pagination, invoice detail hien ten tro.

## Trang thai hien tai
- Manual 2-step: `POST /invoices/generate` (DRAFT) -> `POST /invoices/issue` (ISSUED)
- Chua co `@nestjs/schedule`, khong co cron job nao
- Data san co: Contract.paymentDay, Contract.paymentCycle, MeterReading, Invoice status flow
- Invoice unique constraint: `contractId_invoiceMonth_invoiceYear`
- Invoice list hien tai: flat list, khong group by motel, khong co ten tro
- Invoice detail: khong hien ten tro
- Chua co UI manual generate invoice (chi co API)

## Kien truc

```
Cron (1st of month 00:05)
  |
  v
SchedulerService.handleAutoGenerate()
  |
  v
InvoiceService.autoGenerateForAllMotels()
  |-- Query contracts: status=ACTIVE, paymentCycle match
  |-- For each contract: reuse generate() logic
  |-- Skip if invoice already exists (unique constraint)
  |-- Log results (generated/skipped/failed)
  v
Cron (daily 09:00)
  |
  v
SchedulerService.handleAutoIssue()
  |
  v
InvoiceService.autoIssueReadyInvoices()
  |-- Query invoices: status=DRAFT, autoGenerated=true
  |-- For each: reuse issue() logic
  |-- Log results
```

## Quyet dinh thiet ke
1. **Global schedule** - Chung cho tat ca motel, co the bat/tat per-motel
2. **Lenient generation** - Tao invoice ke ca khi chua co meter reading (dien/nuoc = 0)
3. **Delayed issue** - Generate DRAFT truoc, issue rieng (cho phep review)
4. **System user** - Cron job dung userId = "SYSTEM" de track auto-generated
5. **Per-motel toggle** - Motel co field `autoInvoice: Boolean` de bat/tat
6. **Manual generate UI** - Form tao hoa don (cho truong hop roi tro dot xuat)
7. **Invoice list grouped** - Group by motel voi pagination per section (nhu contracts page)
8. **Invoice detail motel** - Hien ten tro trong info card

## Risk
- Duplicate invoices: Da co unique constraint `contractId_invoiceMonth_invoiceYear`
- Missing meter: Tao invoice voi dien/nuoc = 0, staff update sau
- Server restart: Cron tu dong resume khi app restart (NestJS schedule built-in)
